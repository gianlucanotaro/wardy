PLAN · REDESIGN

Mock-up for issue #75 (epic #74). The week stays a vertical list, but every day becomes a card with readable outfit thumbnails. Tapping a day opens a look picker sheet instead of cycling through looks. Past days say what was worn. Upcoming days have reserved space for the forecast and an occasion. Sample data; today is Thu 12 Nov.

Decisions for you

  1. 1 · One outfit per day, or several? One look per day. The card stays at a fixed 112px and the plan_days table doesn't change. If you need a second outfit for the evening, save it as a look and wear it. Slots per day (DAY / EVENING). The card grows to 176px, the picker asks which slot, and plan_days needs a slot column (schema change). See frame A.
  2. 2 · Unsaved outfits in the plan? Only saved looks. "Plan for…" on Build or a suggestion (#81) saves a look first, with an editable name such as "BUILD · FRI 13 NOV", then plans it. One kind of thing in the plan, no new table. The plan stores the raw outfit and shows it with an UNSAVED tag, plus a "save as look" action. Needs a plan_items table. See frame B.
  3. 3 · Occasion per day? Yes, but later (#82, after the schema approval). It is set at the top of the picker sheet, shown as a small tag under the date, and looks that fit it rank first with a FITS tag. See frames 3 and 4. No column. The picker shows every look in a fixed order and Today keeps its own occasion chips. No migration.

Day card spec (#77)

CardA <button> covering the whole card. Fixed height 112px including the 1px --color-line bottom border (the last card has a 2px --color-line-strong one). Grid 64px 1fr, gap 12, padding 12px 16px. The height is the same in every state, so paging weeks doesn't shift the layout.
Date columnWeekday 800 13px, .08em. Day number 800 30px/30px, -0.02em, tabular-nums.
Title row20px high. Look name 800 15px uppercase, truncated. The right end stays free for the forecast (#80). Past days put "Planned X · wore Y" here (#78).
Thumbnails45×60 (3:4), gap 4px, 1px --color-line-strong border, --color-surface background, object-cover, alt="". Up to 5; with more than 5, show 4 and a "+N" tile.
UnplannedTitle "UNPLANNED" in 800 11px .12em --color-neutral-700. A 60px dashed box with "+ PLAN A LOOK" (800 10.5px, neutral-700). A past day with no plan says "NOTHING PLANNED" and has no box.
Today--color-accent-100 background, a 4px --color-accent inset bar on the left, weekday, number and the TODAY label in --color-accent-700. No solid red fills: white on accent is 3.9:1.
PastText in --color-neutral-700, thumbnails grayscale at .55 opacity (images only). Once the wear is shown (#78), the thumbnails show what was worn, in full colour.
LoadingReal weekday and number. The title is a 120×12 surface bar, plus 4 surface blocks of 45×60. A pulse is allowed, but off under reduced motion.
A11yaria-label like "Today, Thursday 12 November, Navy smart casual. Tap to change". Visible focus: a 2px ink outline, offset -2px. Targets: 112px cards, 44px week arrows, 76px picker rows, 52px sheet actions.
